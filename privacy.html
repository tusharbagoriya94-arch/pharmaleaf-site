Privacy policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what personal information collects on pharmaleaf.in and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your email address (to log you in and keep your library).
  • Your name and mobile number (for the order, the payment receipt and the watermark on your PDFs).
  • Order details: what you bought, amount, Razorpay order and payment IDs, and how many times you downloaded.

What we do not collect

Card, UPI and bank details are entered only in the Razorpay window and are handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy. We do not receive them.

How we use it

To provide your notes, confirm payments, prevent sharing of paid notes, answer support requests and meet legal and tax duties. We do not sell your data and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

Where it is stored

Account and order data are stored with our service providers Supabase (database and file storage) and Razorpay (payments). Data is kept while your account is active and as long as the law requires for payment records.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data by writing to . Payment records that the law requires us to keep will be retained.

Cookies

We use only the browser storage needed to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies.
