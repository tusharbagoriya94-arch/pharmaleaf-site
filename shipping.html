Delivery policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

PharmaLeaf sells only digital products. Nothing is shipped by post or courier.

How delivery works

After a successful payment, the semester appears in My Library immediately (at most a few minutes if the bank confirms late). Each subject PDF is downloaded from there. You can log in with the same email and download again later.

Delivery problems

If your notes do not appear within 24 hours of payment, write to surekhapharmaceuticals@gmail.com with your payment ID. See the Refund policy.
