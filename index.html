Questions

What do I get for one semester?

The complete notes of every theory subject in that semester as separate PDFs (for example, Semester 1 has 6 subjects). Practicals and elective courses are not included.

How do I get the PDFs after paying?

Right after payment you are taken to My Library, where each subject has a Download button. Log in with the same email any time to download again.

Can I share the PDFs?

No. Each copy is for one student and carries the buyer's name, email and order number on every page.

Which syllabus do the notes follow?

The PCI B.Pharm Syllabus 2026 (NEP 2020). PharmaLeaf is not an official PCI or RUHS publication.

Payment done but notes not showing?

Wait one minute and refresh My Library. If they still do not appear, write to us from the Contact page with your payment ID.
