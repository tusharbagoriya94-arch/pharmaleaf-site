About us

Last updated: 31 October 2026

PharmaLeaf makes easy-to-understand study notes for B.Pharm students. Every set of notes follows the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) B.Pharm Syllabus 2026 (NEP 2020), unit by unit and topic by topic, as taught in universities such as RUHS.

What we make

  • Complete theory notes for all 8 semesters of B.Pharm (49 subjects).
  • Written in simple English, with tables, comparison charts, simple diagrams and a quick-revision section for every unit.
  • Delivered as PDF files that students download from their account after payment.

Who we are

PharmaLeaf is run by Tushar Bagoriya from Chittorgarh, Rajasthan 312027, India, with a background in the pharmaceutical industry.

Note

PharmaLeaf is an independent publisher. It is not an official publication of the Pharmacy Council of India, RUHS or any university.
