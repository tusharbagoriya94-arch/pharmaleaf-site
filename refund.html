Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated:

Our notes are digital products delivered instantly, so please read the free sample before buying.

When we refund in full

  • Money was deducted but the semester did not appear in My Library within 24 hours and we cannot fix it.
  • You were charged twice for the same semester.
  • A file in your semester cannot be downloaded or is damaged and we cannot replace it within 3 working days.

When we do not refund

  • After any PDF of the semester has been downloaded, except in the cases above, because the content has been delivered.
  • If you bought the wrong semester and have already downloaded it. If you have not downloaded anything, write to us within 7 days and we will move your purchase to the correct semester or refund it.
  • Accounts blocked for sharing or reselling notes.

Cancellation

An order can be cancelled before payment by closing the payment window. A paid order can be cancelled only under the refund rules above.

How to ask

Write to within 7 days of payment with your registered email and Razorpay payment ID. Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
